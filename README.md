# Search to Pro · Fishing Points

Five ranked ideas for Fishing Points's onboarding and paywall, plus two versions of the flow side by side: one for people who searched "fishbrain" on the App Store, one for "tide charts". Prepared by Adapty.

**→ https://eddieatadapty.github.io/fishing-points-asa-rebuild/** · Apple Ads research: https://asa.adapty.io/fishbrain

Opens on the Overview. `?open=proto` lands on the prototype; `&step=<screen>` opens both phones on one screen.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Use the answers you already collect | CR +10–20% to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for the account after the purchase | CR +5–10% to purchase · ARPU up to +17% |
| 03 | Start with the free week switched on | CR +10–20% to trial or purchase |
| 04 | Show what Free leaves out, in your own forecast | CR +5–15% to purchase |
| 05 | Catch the people who tap Back | CR +5–10% from people who close the paywall |

## What differs by keyword

| Screen | "fishbrain" | "tide charts" |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| App Store | Ad screenshots: Fish activity, nautical charts, tides | Ad screenshots: a tide chart, daily highs and lows, sun and moon |
| Welcome | “Forecasts, maps and tides in one app” · See my forecast | “Tides for your spot, today” · See today’s tides |
| Forecasts | Your eight options, in your order | Tides already ticked and moved to the top |
| Today free | Your Fish activity: today open, the rest of the week locked | Your Tides chart for their spot: today open, the week locked |
| Paywall | Free week on, “Catch More Fish” for their trips | Free week on, a carousel: tides, sun and moon, fish activity |
| On Back | $9.99 Monthly with its 7 free days as a timeline | “Today’s tides stay free”, or Monthly with 7 free days |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Fishing Points's App Store listing, website or in-app screens; Apple Ads figures from Adapty's research. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Fishing Points.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
