What this is

We searched for Fishing Points the way a new user does, then rebuilt what happens after the tap.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. For Fishing Points we looked at two things. First, your app: your recorded iOS onboarding, your App Store listing and fishingpoints.app/premium, the same material as our earlier five-idea audit. Second, the App Store searches that lead to it: 36 Apple Ads keywords in about 25 countries, 7 on your own name, 16 on the category (fishing, tides, tide charts, pesca, mareas…) and 13 on other fishing apps’ names. On one of them you already lead: Adapty’s public Fishbrain research puts you at 77.8% share of voice when people search “Fishbrain”.

What follows is the five ideas for the flow you have, and two keyword flows that show what happens when each search gets its own version. Every one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and all of it is clickable. The prototype uses your design, not ours.
